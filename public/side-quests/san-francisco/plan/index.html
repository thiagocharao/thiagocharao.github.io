Street smarts

Skip on foot

  • Tenderloin, roughly Geary to Market and Van Ness to Mason: day and night. Open drug use and tents, not dangerous as such but grim. Brenda's, Saigon Sandwich, Mensho and Bourbon & Branch sit on its edges: Lyft to the door
  • Civic Center and UN Plaza after dark. City Hall is fine for a photo by day
  • Market St between 5th and 8th after dark, and 6th St south of Market at any hour
  • 16th St Mission BART plaza late. The Mission bars are fine, take a Lyft home from the bar
  • Walking through SoMa from Chase Center at night: take a Lyft instead

Fine at night

  • Marina, Cow Hollow, Union and Chestnut St, Russian Hill, North Beach, the Embarcadero, Hayes Valley
  • Mission Bay around the arena before and after the game, with the crowd
  • Fisherman's Wharf is touristy, not risky

Habits

  • Car break-ins are the city's crime. Nothing visible in a Lyft or any car, not even a jacket
  • Phone in the pocket at kerbs and BART exits, snatching happens from bikes
  • Someone asking for money: a no and keep walking. Do not stop to argue
  • Carry a few dollars in cash for counters, Specs' and Toronado
  • Emergency: 911

Spare time bank

Long walks

  • Lands End and Sutro Baths, coastal trail, about 5 km loop, best bridge view in the city. Bus 38R then a walk, or Lyft 20 min. Andytown coffee and a Palm City hoagie are 10 min south.
  • Presidio Tunnel Tops to Crissy Field, 40 min from the hotel, flat, bridge straight ahead.
  • Twin Peaks at sunset, whole city below. Lyft up, walk down into the Castro.
  • Golden Gate Park, Japanese Tea Garden and the de Young tower (free view, no ticket). Half a day.

  • Haight-Ashbury, 60s counterculture streets, Amoeba Music, then Toronado for a beer. Bus 43 from Lombard.
  • Lombard's crooked block, same street as the hotel, 20 min walk east. Swensen's is two blocks away.
